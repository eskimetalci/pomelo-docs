Pomelo

Focus & Task Management

About

Pomelo is a personal productivity app that combines a Pomodoro timer with task management. It's designed for people who want to work with intention: capture tasks, prioritise them, and stay focused one session at a time.

Features

Pomodoro timer with focus and break cycles · Automatic time logging per session · Task inbox with Eisenhower prioritisation · Today and Tomorrow planning views · Reminders and Calendar integration (iOS) · Fully offline, no account required
