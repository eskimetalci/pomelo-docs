Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Pomelo is a personal productivity app built for individuals who want to manage their tasks and focus sessions. Your privacy is important to us.

No Data Collection

Pomelo does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app operates entirely on your device. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers.

Local Storage Only

All tasks, timer sessions, and preferences are stored locally on your device using on-device databases. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Calendar & Reminders Access

If you grant permission, Pomelo reads your calendar events and reminders to display them alongside your tasks. This data is read locally and is never transmitted anywhere.

Crash Reporting (Sentry)

Pomelo uses Sentry to collect anonymous crash and error reports. When the app crashes or encounters an unexpected error, Sentry receives a report containing:

  • The stack trace (code location of the error)
  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version
  • A randomly generated, anonymous device identifier

Crash reports never include your tasks, timer data, calendar events, or any content you enter in the app. This data is used solely to identify and fix bugs.

Third-Party Services

Sentry is the only third-party service used in Pomelo. No analytics platforms, advertising networks, or data brokers are used. Sentry's own privacy policy is available at sentry.io/privacy.

Children's Privacy

Pomelo does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
